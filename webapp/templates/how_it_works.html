How this app works

Every application moves through the same pipeline. Each stage only unlocks once the one before it is current and, where relevant, you've made the decisions it's asking for.

The pipeline

  1. 1. Evidence Profile

    What it is: the verified facts about you — skills, experience, education, languages — pulled from your CV and other sources.

    Why it matters: everything the app writes about you must trace back to something here. Nothing is invented.

    What to do: set it up once, then keep it updated as your experience changes.

  2. 2. Find/Add Job

    What it is: the job posting you're evaluating, either discovered through search or pasted in directly.

    Why it matters: it's the fixed source text every later stage compares your evidence against.

    What to do: add a posting to start a new application workspace.

  3. 3. Understanding

    What it is: the requirements, responsibilities, and logistics extracted from the posting, each traceable back to an exact quote.

    Why it matters: Job Fit only ever compares against what was actually extracted here — not assumptions about the role.

    What to do: run it once the posting is saved; rerun it if the posting text changes.

  4. 4. Job Fit

    What it is: a structured comparison of your Evidence Profile against the extracted requirements — direct matches, transferable experience, and gaps.

    Why it matters: this is the evidence basis for everything Application Intelligence writes next.

    What to do: review any flagged matches or questions it raises.

  5. 5. Application Intelligence

    What it is: draft CV bullets and cover-letter wording, generated only from evidence Job Fit already accepted.

    Why it matters: it proposes wording, but never decides on your behalf what gets used.

    What to do: read the proposals; nothing is included until you review it.

  6. 6. Review

    What it is: your decisions on every proposal, match, and flagged item — use it or leave it out.

    Why it matters: only what you explicitly approve here can become part of your application pack.

    What to do: work through the outstanding items until none remain.

  7. 7. Application Pack

    What it is: the frozen, reviewed CV and cover-letter content, created once your reviewed material meets the minimum content requirements.

    Why it matters: creating it never submits anything — it only prepares the reviewed content for download.

    What to do: create it once your review queue is empty and the material is ready.

  8. 8. Download

    What it is: the rendered CV and cover-letter documents from your confirmed pack.

    Why it matters: downloading is just retrieving the file — it still isn't submission.

    What to do: download and use them in your actual job application, wherever that happens.

  9. 9. Apply

    What it is: you telling the app you actually submitted the application outside the tool.

    Why it matters: the app never assumes submission on your behalf — you mark it explicitly.

    What to do: mark applied once you've actually sent it.

  10. 10. Track outcome

    What it is: the real-world result — interview, offer, rejection, and so on.

    Why it matters: keeps your pipeline view accurate.

    What to do: update the status as things happen.

Status glossary

StatusWhat it means
CurrentReady to run — nothing is blocking this stage.
Stale / needs updatingSomething it depended on changed since this result was created; it needs to be rerun before it can be trusted.
Needs reviewThe system generated something but wants your explicit decision before it's used.
Blocked / incompleteThis stage can't proceed yet — an earlier requirement (a decision, a rerun, a minimum amount of content) isn't satisfied.
ReadyThis meets every requirement to move forward.
Historical packA CV/cover letter you confirmed previously. It stays downloadable even after later changes make new material stale or incomplete — confirming a new pack never happens automatically.
DraftedYou've confirmed a reviewed application pack (Gate 4), but haven't told the app you actually submitted it yet.
AppliedYou've told the app you submitted this application outside the tool.
