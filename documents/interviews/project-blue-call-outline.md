# Project Blue — Call Outline
### Cheat sheet for tonight. Scan, don't read.

**Hold in your head:**
- You're not declining their job. You're upgrading their plan.
- Sell through the founder who gets it. Address the skeptic's specific fear, not the abstract case.
- The walk-away is your strongest card. Calm beats needy. Neediness collapses the rate.
- Goal tonight: a yes to the next step, not signatures.

---

## 1. Open (warm, in control)
- Don't pitch yet. Get them talking.
- *"Before I tell you how I'd approach this, walk me through what you're actually trying to solve with this hire. What does the next 6 months need to look like?"*

## 2. Discovery (let the gap reveal itself)
Ask, then listen. These are diligence AND they surface the strategy gap on their own.
- *"What's your net revenue retention? Where does churn hide in the annual cohort vs. monthly?"*
- *"When Sendblue or another competitor undercuts you next quarter, what's the answer?"*
- *"How much of the roadmap is in [engineer]'s head vs. written down and prioritized?"*
- *"What's your line-flagging rate, and what happens to a customer when a number dies?"*

If they don't have crisp answers, you just proved the point without saying it.

## 3. The reframe (core move)
- *"You'll want a full-time PM. Just not first."*
- *"A full-time PM needs a roadmap, a strategy, and a surface to manage. You don't have those yet. Hire one now and you're paying full-time for someone to invent the strategy, which mid-level PMs are bad at, while doing ticket work you don't need full-time."*
- *"Let me set positioning, roadmap, and instrumentation in 90 days. Then you either don't need a full-time PM, or you know exactly who to hire and I help you hire them."*
- You're making their plan succeed later and cheaper. You're on their side.

## 4. Rate (don't blink, then stop talking)
- *"It's $15k a month. That's the same spend you'd put on the full-time PM, except senior, no ramp, no management load, scoped to outcomes."*
- (Alt if they want flexible capacity: *"Or $2,500 a day in blocks."*)
- Lead the kicker as commitment proof: *"And I'll tie part of my pay to net-new MRR. I want skin in your growth, not just a retainer."*
- Say the number. Then be quiet. Let them react first.

## 5. Objections → responses
**"We were leaning full-time PM."**
→ *"Totally. And in 6 months you probably should. I'm how you get to the point of hiring the right one instead of guessing now."*

**"You're overqualified. Why would you do this?"**
→ *"Because it's a clean, defined outcome I can deliver fast, and I run a portfolio. This fits how I work. You get the senior version without paying for full-time."*

**"$15k is more than the salary."**
→ *"Per month it's the same once you load the salary with benefits, equity, and ramp. You're just not carrying a full-time seat or three months of someone getting up to speed."*

**"Can you just go full-time?"**
→ *"I'm not taking a full-time seat right now. But you don't need me full-time to get this done, and I'd rather earn the next phase than lock you in before there's proof."*

**"What about commitment if you've got other things going?"**
→ *"That's what the performance kicker answers. I'm betting my own pay on your MRR. And the work is outcome-scoped with weekly proof, so you'll see it, not just trust it."*

## 6. If Warmode comes up (disclosure-first, don't bundle)
- *"Separate topic, and I'll be upfront. I co-found Warmode. There may be a fit down the line, but I'm not bundling it into this. If we ever explore it, here's the boundary memo I've already written to protect you from my own conflict."*
- Then redirect: *"Let's land this engagement on its own merits first."*

## 7. Your situation (say it before they ask)
- *"For full transparency: I run Warmode and Omega Point, and I'm evaluating select full-time roles. This engagement delivers a defined outcome regardless, and we'll have notice terms if anything changes. Founders hiring fractional expect this."*

## 8. Close (concrete next step)
- *"Here's what I'd do: a 45-minute working session to lock the four outcomes, then I send a one-page SOW. I can have the plan to you this week."*
- Get the yes to the session. That's the win tonight.

## 9. Walk-away (in reserve, deliver calm)
- *"If what you need is a full-time body in the seat, I'm probably the wrong fit, and that's fine. No hard feelings."*
- Use it if they keep pushing full-time after two reframes. It resets the power in the room.

---

**Do:** ask first, anchor firm, go silent after the number, sell through your champion, name the walk.
**Don't:** pitch before they talk, justify your rate unprompted, bundle Warmode, sound eager, accept "full-time or nothing" as the only frame.
