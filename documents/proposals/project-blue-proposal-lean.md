# Proposal: Fractional Growth Engagement
**Omega Point × Project Blue** · July 2026

## Engagement

Omega Point (AJ Magnuson) will serve as Project Blue's fractional growth lead: build the growth measurement infrastructure, own the growth roadmap, and establish the product build cadence — in support of Project Blue's goal of doubling MRR by end of year.

## Scope

**Growth infrastructure**
- Instrument analytics for North Star (MRR) progress and core growth metrics (AARRR: acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, referral), including:
  - Revenue split by annual vs. monthly
  - 1-line vs. 2+ line accounts
  - Upsell line tracking
- Verify a clean recurring-MRR baseline (subscription vs. invoice revenue separated)
- Build/co-build a system for mining sales and CX calls for product feedback and missing features, tied to lifecycle stages
- Spend-readiness assessment: what must be true before scaling marketing spend efficiently, and current state against it

**Growth leadership**
- Strategy advisory
- Growth model and competitive analysis
- Propose and prioritize the product & growth roadmap; execution by the engineering team through an established PM/engineer build cadence
- Recommendation on the ideal future PM hire profile, informed by the build cadence

## Structure

- **Capacity:** ~2 days/week equivalent, outcome-oriented
- **Cadence:** Weekly working session with founders + engineer; async otherwise; weekly written progress updates
- **Term:** 90-day initial commitment, then ongoing month-to-month
- **Exit:** Day-45 checkpoint: formal review of progress and fit. At the checkpoint, either party may elect to conclude the engagement, effective day 60, with documented handoff; performance bonus tranches lapse on early conclusion. Otherwise the term runs through day 90, after which either party may end the engagement with 30 days' written notice.
- **Non-exclusive:** Omega Point maintains other clients and projects.

## Fees

- **Retainer:** $15,000/month, invoiced monthly in advance
- **Performance bonus (MRR-based), two tranches:**
  - *Day 90 — on-pace milestone.* One-time bonus tied to net-new recurring MRR (new minus churned) above the verified baseline. Illustrative: $10,000 at +$50k net-new.
  - *December 31 — the goal itself.* One-time bonus tied to Project Blue's stated objective of doubling MRR by year end, measured against the verified recurring baseline. Illustrative: $15,000 at +$100k net-new; $30,000 at a full 2x of baseline (cap).
  - Because current exact MRR must first be verified, final figures are set jointly by day 21 — after the baseline data dive — and documented in a one-page addendum. Mechanics fixed now: verified recurring-MRR baseline by end of week 2 (subscription vs. invoice revenue separated); both tranches capped; measured on the dashboards built under this engagement. Both tranches survive any termination of the engagement provided the initial 90-day term was completed, and are measured on the scheduled dates regardless of engagement status.
- Tool/SaaS costs paid directly by Project Blue; all analytics accounts and infrastructure created under Project Blue's ownership from day one.

## Client dependencies

- Access to billing/revenue data ([Stripe / GoHighLevel / other]) and relevant systems
- Engineer availability per the agreed build cadence
- Sales/CX call recordings (if not currently recorded, enabling recording is a prerequisite for the call-mining workstream)
- One weekly founder session; a designated decision-maker

## Terms

- All work product created for Project Blue belongs to Project Blue. Omega Point retains its pre-existing methods and frameworks, and may reference the engagement as a case study with Project Blue's approval on any figures shared.
- Upon any conclusion of the engagement, Omega Point provides documented handoff of all systems, dashboards, and work in progress.
- Mutual NDA (yours or ours).
- Invoiced by [Omega Point entity].
- Start date: within one week of acceptance, subject to data access.
