# Writing Policy

- Use warm, direct, conversational professional language.
- Use active voice and concrete evidence.
- Do not use em-dashes, cliches, filler, generic buzzwords, or apologetic hedging.
- Reframe emphasis, never substance. Do not claim an adjacent skill as direct experience.
- Every company-specific claim must be independently verified from supplied research sources.
- Keep cover letters forward-looking: explain which employer tasks the candidate can solve and how.
- Address every stated requirement honestly. Gaps are acknowledged and bridged with adjacent experience, never hidden or stuffed with keywords.
- Match the cover-letter language to the posting. Keep CV language at the profile-level setting.
- Preserve factual grounding against the master CV and approved profile. Dates, employers, titles, metrics, technologies, education, and certifications must not drift.
- Any mention of agentic coding or AI tooling must use only a fact supported by the approved profile.

## Cover-letter structure

1. Role-specific opening and strongest relevant connection.
2. Why this company, based on verified research.
3. Forward-looking task-solving evidence, with three to five concrete bullets when useful.
4. Personal/team fit grounded in approved behavioral signals.
5. Brief confident closing.

Target one page and approximately 250-300 words of body text.
