No formal assessment on file - this profile is a **self-assessment synthesized from the /setup interview (Aug 2026)** and corroborating evidence from the CV. Samuel presents a **self-directed builder with an executor-bridge pattern**: he teaches himself new tooling fast, ships concrete artifacts, and in teams naturally executes and connects people rather than seeking the spotlight.

- **Shipping working systems:** Turns vague quality problems into concrete, reusable tooling - the automated RAG evaluation pipeline ended in a report with recommendations, not just a test run.
- **Bridging & organizing:** Ran a successful election campaign (36.4% voting rate), organized society events with positive department feedback, and led 12 freshmen through a 200+ person orientation camp.

## How You Work Best

- Clear goals with freedom to choose the approach; access to experienced engineers for mentorship
- Variety in the week: building, testing, learning - not a single repeated task

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "startup", "fast-paced", "hands-on", "ownership", "0 to 1"
- "LLM", "RAG", "AI evaluation", "AI quality", "agent testing"
- "learning opportunities", "mentorship", "work with experienced engineers"
- "automation", "tooling", "build internal tools"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "manual testing" as the whole role, "data entry", "data labeling" as the sole duty
- "repetitive", "routine", "well-established processes" with no building component
- "rigid hierarchy", heavily siloed teams, maintenance-only work

## Management Style Preferences
- Works best with: clear objectives, autonomy on the how, regular feedback, mentors who review his work and push him
- Doesn't work: micromanagement, task queues with no context, roles where learning stops after onboarding
