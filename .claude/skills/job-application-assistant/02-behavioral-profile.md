Flag as **potential friction** (not a deal-breaker):

  management role would be a genuine change of direction, worth being deliberate about.

  mispriced or read as overqualified.
- "fast-paced, ship-first, move-fast-and-break-things" — the demonstrated track record is
  reliability-oriented in regulated domains; not a mismatch, but worth probing what they mean.
- "on-site" / "hybrid" / "must be located in <metro>" — hard constraint conflict, see 04.
- Heavy front-end-only or mobile roles — TypeScript/Angular is real but secondary to backend.
