# Perplexity's Position
Perplexity has turned upmarket and it is finding success. Differentiators that drive enterprise conversions include (1) model agnosticism and (2) citation-based result accuracy.

### **Model Agnosticism**
Perplexity has been characterized as middleware AI with narrowing strategic terrain. The frontier companies OpenAI and Anthropic are shipping more features and products in the application layer, and there is some risk in dependency on access and cost of their models. Some have claimed that orchestration will be increasingly commoditized.

However, there are a number of tailwinds that make the adoption of model agnostic AI solutions rationally more attractive: 
- unpredictable, often exorbitant token expenses of frontier models
- near weekly jockeying of pole position of most powerful model
- quality improvements of cheap, open source models

The promises of AI have outpaced perceptions of its risks, limitations, and barriers to real outcomes. Beyond expenses, cautionary tales of hallucinations, slop, and security failures are beginning to proliferate, steering the discourse towards emphasis on trust, accuracy, and governance.

**Strategic takeaway:** Contribute to market education on the dependency risks of a single frontier company. 

### **Citation-forward**
Citation is a Perplexity differentiator that selects its own market. Citations matter most where stakes are highest: decision-makers whose choices have outsized impact, and experts whose published claims have outsized reputational cost. This mechanically defines an ICP with potent real world influence, and the early success of the upmarket shift hints at an advantageous bidirectional pull.

**Strategic takeaway:** Citation as a differentiator aligns Perplexity with a wedge of people whose output carries disproportionate weight in the world.

### **Capex vs Orchestration**
Because Perplexity is model-agnostic, it bears no frontier-training capex. This creates room for differentiated pricing, tiering, and potentially margin superiority. Pricing and margins can serve as GTM levers and reinforce differentiation.

**Strategic takeaway:** Construct enterprise pricing to counter the dependency risks (ex. savings of orchestration are passed on to the customer).

### **Elevated Brand Value**
Perplexity's differentiators are not mere value propositions, but pillars of the brand. An association with trust, accuracy, and governance is valuable in its own right--these are the very qualities that will appeal to the "impact decision-maker" identified as the buyer ICP--those who bear the highest stakes for impact and reputation. 

However, thanks to early consumer traction in AI search, Perplexity's brand power does not stop there. Perplexity is frequently mentioned among an elite set of AI companies alongside OpenAI, Anthropic, and Cursor. Since this elevated brand rests on consumer reach that may have slowed, this may be a temporary state. 

**Strategic takeaway:** Now, while prestige outpaces market share, Perplexity's brand value ought to be aggressively channeled towards new markets (enterprise) and towards solidifying the trust, accuracy, and governance identity.

### **Reflexive GTM**
Perplexity rightfully biases to build over buy. It is among the rare class of companies that can use its own product to build its tools. This is a compounding benefit:

- cost savings on tools and tool-creation tools (AI) - medium benefit
- daily sensitivity with the power and limitations of the product ("dog-fooding") - high benefit
- forcing function to trailblaze its own success studies in a white hot AI use case (GTM) - highest benefit

The reflexive nature of this opportunity can be a bit conceptually dizzying, but it amounts to Perplexity using its own product to build its own GTM tools which will convert new Perplexity users by showcasing the success of its GTM tools built on Perplexity. This is a self-reinforcing loop, as the more effective the GTM system, the more successful the GTM, the more powerful the case study, the greater the conversion rate, the more users will adopt Perplexity for GTM, the more Perplexity can learn from its users, the more effective it can make its tools... and so on. We already have a sterling example in the [Perplexity: Ramp case study](https://www.perplexity.ai/enterprise/customers/ramp).

This loop thrives on excellence of the system, the execution, and effectiveness in communicating success.

Perplexity's current GTM stack already points to a modern engineer-forward reverse ETL system:

# The State of GTM
The GTM space has evolved in phases over the past decade, replacing playbooks at each phase; however, AI has caused at least four simultaneous disruptions that may retire the concept of playbooks indefinitely:

- Build over Buy: dramatic swing towards building software inhouse over buying it
- SaaS pricing: pressures toward less predictable new pricing models such as usage-based 
- Channel saturation: AI-facilitated output volume means more competition for reach and awareness
- AI-mediated discovery: enhanced guidance on fit puts pressure on pipeline accuracy and AEO

These and other factors change the GTM dynamic. The environment invalidates playbooks every 6-8 weeks while rewarding agile, experiment-driven GTM systems. This is reminiscent of the shift towards PLG with its data-rigorous methodologies and emphasis towards velocity but now with the power of agents and new measurement systems at our disposal.

We need to envision future-proof GTM systems from first principles. This is a stack founded on strict measurement or inference of incrementality. Maximum growth is not about finding the right tactics within the right channels, but finding and extracting value from those tactic / channel pairs in the precise window when they are relevant. Learning, capitalizing, and sunsetting channel tactics is now core to the monthly discipline. 

On the flip side, owned reach increases in leverage, so supplementing agile experimentation with an accruing asset of predictable engagement--active users, email subscribers, content audiences--is another crucial pillar. 

These pillars are not new, but the formulas are more brutal. Reduced barriers to entry --> increased competition --> razor thin margin of error --> pressure on excellence in systems and methods. We need to build the right abstractions to account for this, because while the pace of change for tools and methods is the fastest it has ever been, it is at the slowest it ever will be. 

GTM voices are converging on the same components:
- **Owned, high-quality artifacts** (Spaces, changelogs, research reports, templates) engineered for AI discoverability, not just Google.
- **Individual-brand distribution** — founders, engineers, and functional experts posting under their own names, not the company page.
- **Signal-triggered, human-in-the-loop outbound** replacing volume sequences.
- **Product-led loops** measured by trial-to-first-value, not MQL count.
- **Consumption / outcome pricing** alongside or replacing per-seat pricing.
- **Incrementality testing** (geo, holdout, matched cohort, MMM) replacing multi-touch attribution.    
- **Developer or community substrate** — the durable moat that compounds regardless of channel volatility.

**Strategic takeaways:** the future-proof GTM system is anti-fragile, built on a foundation of incrementality measurement and agile Perplexity-generated tooling
# Parallel Phenomenon: the "pedagogical singularity"
I want to introduce another compounding layer: the "pedagogical singularity" (this is a placeholder name, to be clear):

The creatorsphere remains in a state of explosive growth. This will continue. 
Content has evolved alongside monetization. Educational content has emerged as a winner for its strengths in retention and monetization (courses, coaching, community, brand deals, etc.). Many education creators are not true experts or leaders of their field. They are (1) entrepreneurial, (2) skilled at content production, and (3) tolerant of the risks and compensation of the creator profession. The advantages conferred by (1) and (2) are eroding, with AI contributing especially to the drop in barrier to satisfactory content production (2). For (3), the iterative R&D for content monetization will approach a tipping point where expected value of a reasonably consistent domain expert could compete with other compensation, such as institutional salaries. Volatility of the economy and pressures on growth through owned reach will intensify the appeal.

The collateral of a domain expert creator is content that transfers information, knowledge, and skills in an efficient, digestible way. The organized thinking of LLMs--with language, design, research capabilities--catalyzes iterative knowledge delivery by an order of magnitude. The breadth and depth of memetic knowledge is undergoing rapid expansion. Today, primer knowledge and entry level skills are accessible in an instant. However, more complex knowledge, 2nd order skills, or non-obvious syntheses of concepts is the next frontier of knowledge delivery. As audience appetites graduate from 1st order to 2nd order educational content and beyond, true experts will be increasingly drawn in with greater awareness of opportunities, incentives, and advantages in the pursuit. 

Perplexity has a clear role to play here. 
Citations are critical for high stakes "reads"--the use of cited knowledge to inform a decision-maker who needs trust in accuracy of the information. However, it is the "writes"--where reputational risk lives--that hold a promise of exponential alpha if Perplexity can corner this high expert wedge and harness the creation of content purposed for the user's reach towards product and brand growth.

**Strategic takeaways:** Perplexity's brand and differentiators make it a strategic imperative to vie for experts' public-facing content production. Experts triple-count as buyers, influencers, and reach. Treat "writes" as the KPI: maximize impressions of Perplexity-facilitated information in the world.

# Bones of Strategy

Perplexity's position and the new realities of GTM suggest some contours of a structural strategy.

- **Model agnosticism:** Contribute to market education on the dependency risks of a single frontier company.
- **Citation-forward:** Citation aligns Perplexity with a wedge of people whose output carries disproportionate weight in the world.
- **Capex vs orchestration:** Construct enterprise pricing to counter dependency risks (savings of orchestration passed to the customer).
- **Elevated brand value:** Now, while prestige outpaces market share, channel brand value aggressively toward enterprise and toward the trust/accuracy/governance identity.
- **Reflexive GTM:** Build-in-public motion and a well-honed case-study flywheel.
- **Anti-fragile system:** Incrementality measurement at the foundation, Perplexity-generated tooling throughout.
- **Luminary wedge:** Experts triple-count as buyers, influencers, and reach. Treat "writes" as the KPI: maximize impressions of Perplexity-facilitated information in the world.

# An Illustrative "Big Bet"

Drawing from several of the strategy points, I went through an exercise of envisioning a "bold bet" for the purposes of discussion, to illustrate expansive thinking within structural growth constraints. In a real execution, something like this would be slated for discussion towards the end of the 90 day plan, after larger risks are earned, and it would likely come out quite different after 3 months of learning.

Perplexity Studios is a program to turn Perplexity Computer into a complete personal-branding content motion for experts. This includes voice / style configuration, calendar and cadence management, high-production outputs such as professionally edited video, and a feedback loop integrated with channel analytics. It comprises mostly tools built on or integrated with Perplexity Computer. Integrations would include Eleven Labs, Suno, Descript, etc.
We would incubate this program with Perplexity employees to run a modified version of "individual-brand" distribution, to grow their own audiences in their chosen domain, not to merely distribute or repost Perplexity marketing content. 
Later, we would roll out to "luminaries", or domain experts, to establish their content motions. We could scale luminaries through partnerships with publishers, talent agencies, academic institutions, Substack, etc. 
The intent would be to capture the "luminary" wedge for high reach content production, vying for that high visibility use case similarly to how Anthropic vied for programming.

Content production is often the first celebrated use case for OpenClaw, Claude Cowork and similar systems, but set up is still elaborate and outputs are generic, flat-medium (text, image), and slop-prone. Perplexity's unique advantage for this use case comes directly from its enterprise differentiators: multi-modal model neutrality and citations to curb slop and hallucination. Luminaries represent a power law reach and influence segment, and trends suggest that the domain influencers of the next 5 years have likely not even begun posting content yet.

Success measurement:
- Quantitative: increased retention, referral-driven acquisition, conversion of "luminaries"
- Qualitative: user feedback, Perplexity tutorials in the wild
